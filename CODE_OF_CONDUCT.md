# Code of conduct

These repos follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be patient and kind. Many people here are new to Hackintosh, and many don't write English as a first language.
- Talk about the problem, not the person. Disagree with a fix or a decision, but don't insult anyone for it.
- No harassment, slurs, sexual content, or personal attacks, in issues, discussions, PRs, commits or anywhere else connected to these repos.
- Don't post other people's private information: their logs with serials or addresses, their real name, where they live.
- Help requests aren't orders. Nobody owes anyone a fix or a deadline.

If someone breaks these rules, email admin@watchthelight.org, or use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment. I'll read every report and keep who sent it to me confidential. Depending on what happened I may edit or hide comments, lock a thread, or block someone from the org.

This applies to everyone, me included.
